Question

Nandini has 25 marbles, of which 15 are white. What percentage of her marbles are white?
(i) 10% (ii) 15% (iii) 25% (iv) 60% (v) 40% (vi) None of these
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Total marbles = 25
White marbles = 15
Fraction of white marbles = whitetotal = 1525
To get a percentage, multiply by 100%: 1525 × 100%
100 ÷ 25 = 4, so this is 15 × 4% = 60%
Easy check: 25 marbles is one quarter of 100. So multiply everything by 4: 15 white out of 25 is the same as 60 white out of 100.

60% of the marbles are white – option (iv).
