Question

The price of a mobile phone is ₹8,250. A GST of 18% is added. Which of the following gives the final price including GST?
(i) 8250 × 0.18 (ii) 8250 × 18 (iii) 8250 + 0.18 (iv) 8250 × 0.82 (v) 8250 × 1.18 (vi) 8250 + 8250 × 0.18
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Price = ₹8,250; GST = 18% of 8,250 = 18100 × 8,250 = 8,250 × 0.18 = ₹1,485
Final price = price + GST = 8,250 + 8,250 × 0.18 = ₹9,735
Shortcut: paying 100% + 18% = 118% → 8,250 × 1.18 = ₹9,735

Options (v) 8250 × 1.18 and (vi) 8250 + 8250 × 0.18 are correct; the final price is ₹9,735.
