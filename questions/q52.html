Question

In a room of 100 people, 99% are left-handed. How many left-handed people have to leave the room to bring that percentage down to 98%?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Left-handed = 99% of 100 = 99 people; right-handed = 1 person.
Let x left-handed people leave.
Left-handed remaining = 99 − x; total remaining = 100 − x
We want 99 − x100 − x = 98% = 98100
Cross-multiply: 100(99 − x) = 98(100 − x)
9900 − 100x = 9800 − 98x
9900 − 9800 = 100x − 98x → 100 = 2x → x = 50
Easier way: the 1 right-handed person must become 2% of the room, so the room must have 50 people. 100 − 50 = 50 must leave.

50 left-handed people must leave (surprising, but true!).
