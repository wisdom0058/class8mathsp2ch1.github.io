Question

1600 people voted in an election and the winner got 500 votes. What percent of the total votes did the winner get? Can you guess the minimum number of candidates who stood for the election?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Total votes = 1600, winner's votes = 500
Winner's percent = 5001600 × 100% = 516 × 100% = 50016% = 31.25%

Minimum number of candidates

The winner has the most votes. Every other candidate has at most 500 votes (actually fewer).
Remaining votes = 1600 − 500 = 1100. If there were only 2 other candidates they could have at most 500 + 500 = 1000 < 1100, not enough.
With 3 other candidates: at most 1500 ≥ 1100, possible (e.g. 500, 400, 400, 300).

The winner got 31.25% of the votes; there must have been at least 4 candidates.
