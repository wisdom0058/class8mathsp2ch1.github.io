Question

The post office offers interest of 7% p.a. How much interest would one get on ₹50,000 for 3 years without compounding? How much more with compounding?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Without compounding

Interest per year = 7% of 50,000 = 7100 × 50,000 = ₹3,500
Interest for 3 years = 3 × 3,500 = ₹10,500; amount = ₹60,500

With compounding

Year 1: 50,000 × 1.07 = 53,500
Year 2: 53,500 × 1.07 = 57,245
Year 3: 57,245 × 1.07 = 61,252.15
Compound interest = 61,252.15 − 50,000 = ₹11,252.15

Extra due to compounding

11,252.15 − 10,500 = ₹752.15

Interest without compounding ₹10,500; with compounding about ₹752 more.
