Question

Find the value of the following and also draw their bar models.
(i) 25% of 160 (ii) 16% of 250 (iii) 62% of 360 (iv) 140% of 40 (v) 1% of 1 hour (vi) 7% of 10 kg
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

How to find a percent of a quantity

x% of a quantity = x100 × quantity. Always try to simplify before multiplying.

(i) 25% of 160

25% = 25100 = 14 (one quarter)
14 × 160 = 160 ÷ 4 = 40

(ii) 16% of 250

16100 × 250 — 250 ÷ 100 = 2.5, so this is 16 × 2.5
16 × 2.5 = 40

(iii) 62% of 360

62100 × 360 — 360 ÷ 100 = 3.6, so this is 62 × 3.6
62 × 3.6 = 62 × 3 + 62 × 0.6 = 186 + 37.2 = 223.2

(iv) 140% of 40

140100 × 40 — 40 ÷ 100 = 0.4, so this is 140 × 0.4
140 × 0.4 = 56 (more than 40, because 140% is more than the whole)

(v) 1% of 1 hour

1 hour = 60 minutes = 60 × 60 = 3600 seconds
1100 × 3600 seconds = 36 seconds

(vi) 7% of 10 kg

10 kg = 10 × 1000 g = 10,000 g
7100 × 10,000 g = 7 × 100 g = 700 g (= 0.7 kg)

(i) 40 (ii) 40 (iii) 223.2 (iv) 56 (v) 36 seconds (vi) 700 g
