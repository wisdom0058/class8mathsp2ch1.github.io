Question

Find the missing numbers. The first problem has been worked out.
(i) A bar of 5 equal parts = 100%; 1 part = 20%. If the whole bar is 75, 60 is ___ % (worked out: 4 parts = 80%? – see solution).
(ii) A bar of 10 equal parts; 1 part = ___ %; whole = 90, 6 parts = ___.
(iii) A bar of 4 equal parts; 1 part = ___ %; whole = 140, 3 parts = ___.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Idea

A bar model cuts the whole (100%) into equal parts. The percent of one part = 100% ÷ (number of parts).

(i) 5 equal parts

One part = 100% ÷ 5 = 20%
Whole = 75, so one part = 75 ÷ 5 = 15
4 parts = 4 × 15 = 60, and 4 parts = 4 × 20% = 80%. So 60 is 80% of 75.

(ii) 10 equal parts

One part = 100% ÷ 10 = 10%
Whole = 90, so one part = 90 ÷ 10 = 9
6 parts = 6 × 9 = 54, which is 6 × 10% = 60% of 90.

(iii) 4 equal parts

One part = 100% ÷ 4 = 25%
Whole = 140, so one part = 140 ÷ 4 = 35
3 parts = 3 × 35 = 105, which is 3 × 25% = 75% of 140.

(i) 20%; 60 (ii) 10%; 54 (iii) 25%; 105
