Question

Fill in the blanks appropriately:
(i) 30% of k is 70; 60% of k is ___, 90% of k is ___, 120% of k is ___
(ii) 100% of m is 215; 10% of m is ___, 1% of m is ___, 6% of m is ___
(iii) 90% of n is 270; 9% of n is ___, 18% of n is ___, 100% of n is ___
(iv) Make 2 more such questions.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

(i) 30% of k is 70

60% is 2 times 30%, so 60% of k = 2 × 70 = 140
90% is 3 times 30%, so 90% of k = 3 × 70 = 210
120% is 4 times 30%, so 120% of k = 4 × 70 = 280

(ii) 100% of m is 215

10% is 100% ÷ 10, so 10% of m = 215 ÷ 10 = 21.5
1% is 100% ÷ 100, so 1% of m = 215 ÷ 100 = 2.15
6% is 6 times 1%, so 6% of m = 6 × 2.15 = 12.9

(iii) 90% of n is 270

9% is 90% ÷ 10, so 9% of n = 270 ÷ 10 = 27
18% is 2 times 9%, so 18% of n = 2 × 27 = 54
10% is 90% ÷ 9, so 10% of n = 270 ÷ 9 = 30; then 100% of n = 10 × 30 = 300

(iv) Two more questions (with answers)

25% of x is 50 → 50% of x = 100, 75% of x = 150, 200% of x = 400.
80% of y is 160 → 10% of y = 20, 5% of y = 10, 100% of y = 200.

(i) 140, 210, 280 (ii) 21.5, 2.15, 12.9 (iii) 27, 54, 300
