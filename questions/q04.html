1.1 Fractions as Percentages

Question

A group of friends is participating in a long-distance run. Their positions after 15 minutes are shown on a line from Start to Finish: A is a little before the one-fourth mark, B is a little more than one-third, C is about three-fourths, and D is almost at the finish. Match what percentage of the race each has approximately completed from: 55%, 20%, 38%, 72%, 84%, 93%.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution




The whole race from Start to Finish is 100%. Halfway is 50%, one-fourth is 25%, three-fourths is 75%.
A is before the one-fourth (25%) mark → the only option below 25% is 20%.
B is between one-fourth (25%) and half (50%) → 38%.
C is a little less than three-fourths (75%) → 72%.
D is very close to the finish (100%) → 93%.

A ≈ 20%, B ≈ 38%, C ≈ 72%, D ≈ 93%.
