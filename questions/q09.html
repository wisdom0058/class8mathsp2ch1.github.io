1.2 Percentage of Some Quantity

Question

Write >, <, = in the boxes. Visualising or estimating can help; compute only if necessary.
(i) 50% of 510 ___ 50% of 515
(ii) 37% of 148 ___ 73% of 148
(iii) 29% of 43 ___ 92% of 110
(iv) 30% of 40 ___ 40% of 50
(v) 45% of 200 ___ 10% of 490
(vi) 30% of 80 ___ 24% of 64
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

(i)

Same percent (50%) of two numbers – the bigger number gives the bigger answer. 510 < 515.
50% of 510 < 50% of 515

(ii)

Same number (148) – the bigger percent gives the bigger answer. 37% < 73%.
37% of 148 < 73% of 148

(iii)

29% is smaller than 92% AND 43 is smaller than 110, so the left side is smaller both ways.
Check: 29% of 43 ≈ 12.5 and 92% of 110 ≈ 101.
29% of 43 < 92% of 110

(iv)

30% is smaller than 40% AND 40 is smaller than 50.
Check: 30% of 40 = 12 and 40% of 50 = 20.
30% of 40 < 40% of 50

(v)

45% of 200 = 45100 × 200 = 45 × 2 = 90
10% of 490 = 49
90 > 49, so 45% of 200 > 10% of 490

(vi)

30% of 80 = 30100 × 80 = 3 × 8 = 24
24% of 64 = 24100 × 64 = 15.36
24 > 15.36, so 30% of 80 > 24% of 64

(i) < (ii) < (iii) < (iv) < (v) > (vi) >
