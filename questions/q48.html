Question

Bus fares were increased by 3% last year and by 4% this year. What is the overall percentage increase in the last 2 years?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Let the fare two years ago be ₹100.
After 3% increase: 100 × 1.03 = ₹103
After 4% increase (on ₹103, not on ₹100): 103 × 1.04 = 103 + 4.12 = ₹107.12
Total increase = 107.12 − 100 = ₹7.12 on ₹100
Overall increase = 7.12% (a little more than 3 + 4 = 7%, because the second increase acts on a bigger fare).

Overall increase is 7.12%.
