Question

A shopkeeper set the price of a product with a 35% profit margin. Due to poor sales he offered a 30% discount on the selling price. Will he make a profit or a loss? Give reasons.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Let the cost price be ₹100 (easy to work with).
Marked price with 35% profit = 100 + 35 = ₹135
Discount = 30% of 135 = 30100 × 135 = ₹40.50
Final selling price = 135 − 40.50 = ₹94.50
94.50 is less than the cost 100, so there is a loss of 100 − 94.50 = ₹5.50, i.e. 5.5%.
Reason: the 30% discount is calculated on the bigger amount (₹135), not on the cost (₹100). 30% of 135 is ₹40.50, which is more than the ₹35 profit that was added.

He makes a loss of 5.5%, because the discount is taken on the higher marked price and wipes out more than the added profit.
