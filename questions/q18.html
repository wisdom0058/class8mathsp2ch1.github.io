Question

A shopkeeper buys a geometry box for ₹75 and sells it for ₹110. What is his profit margin with respect to the cost?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Cost price (CP) = ₹75
Selling price (SP) = ₹110
Profit = SP − CP = 110 − 75 = ₹35
Profit % = ProfitCP × 100% = 3575 × 100%
Simplify 3575 by dividing top and bottom by 5: 715
715 × 100% = 70015% = 46.67%

Profit margin ≈ 46.67% of the cost.
