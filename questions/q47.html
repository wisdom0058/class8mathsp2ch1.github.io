Question

A shopkeeper sells pencils such that the selling price of 3 pencils equals the cost price of 5 pencils. Does he make a profit or a loss? What is the percentage?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Let the cost price of 1 pencil be ₹1. Then CP of 5 pencils = ₹5.
SP of 3 pencils = CP of 5 pencils = ₹5
So SP of 1 pencil = 53 rupees ≈ ₹1.67
SP (₹1.67) is more than CP (₹1), so it is a profit.
Profit per pencil = 53 − 1 = 23 rupee
Profit % = ProfitCP × 100% = 23 × 100% = 2003% = 6623%

He makes a profit of 6623% (about 66.7%).
