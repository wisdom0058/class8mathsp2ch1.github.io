1.1 Fractions as Percentages

Question

Express the following fractions as percentages.
(i) 35 (ii) 74 (iii) 920 (iv) 72150 (v) 13 (vi) 511
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

What does percentage mean?

Percent means out of 100. To change a fraction into a percentage we multiply it by 100 and put the % sign.

(i) 35

35 × 100% — divide 100 by 5 first: 100 ÷ 5 = 20
= 3 × 20% = 60%

(ii) 74

74 × 100% — 100 ÷ 4 = 25
= 7 × 25% = 175% (more than 100% because the fraction is more than 1)

(iii) 920

920 × 100% — 100 ÷ 20 = 5
= 9 × 5% = 45%

(iv) 72150

First make the fraction simple: divide top and bottom by 6 → 72150 = 1225
1225 × 100% — 100 ÷ 25 = 4
= 12 × 4% = 48%

(v) 13

13 × 100% = 1003%
100 ÷ 3 = 33 with remainder 1, so 1003 = 3313
= 3313% (about 33.3%)

(vi) 511

511 × 100% = 50011%
500 ÷ 11 = 45 with remainder 5, so 50011 = 45511
= 45511% (about 45.5%)

(i) 60% (ii) 175% (iii) 45% (iv) 48% (v) 3313% (vi) 45511%
