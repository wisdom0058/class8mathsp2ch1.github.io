Question

In a school, 15 of the 80 students come to school by walking. What percentage of the students come by walking?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Total students = 80
Students who walk = 15
Fraction who walk = 1580
Simplify by dividing top and bottom by 5: 1580 = 316
Percentage = 316 × 100% = 30016%
300 ÷ 16 = 18.75

18.75% of the students walk to school (a little less than 20%).
