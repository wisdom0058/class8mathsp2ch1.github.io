Question

Is 10% of a day longer than 1% of a week? Create such questions and challenge your peers.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

1 day = 24 hours
10% of a day = 10100 × 24 hours = 24 ÷ 10 = 2.4 hours
1 week = 7 days = 7 × 24 = 168 hours
1% of a week = 1100 × 168 hours = 1.68 hours
2.4 hours is more than 1.68 hours.

Yes, 10% of a day (2.4 h) is longer than 1% of a week (1.68 h). More such questions: Is 10% of a month (30 days) less than 50% of a week? Is 50% of a dozen more than 10% of a score?
