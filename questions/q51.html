Question

Three shops sell the same item at the same price. Shop A: “Buy 1 get 1 free”; Shop B: “Buy 2 get 1 free”; Shop C: “Buy 3 get 1 free”.
(i) If one item costs ₹100, what is the effective price per item in each shop? Arrange from cheapest to costliest.
(ii) Find the percentage discount in each shop.
(iii) If you need 4 items, which shop would you choose and why?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

(i) Effective price per item

Shop A: pay for 1, get 2 → 1002 = ₹50 per item
Shop B: pay for 2, get 3 → 2003 ≈ ₹66.67 per item
Shop C: pay for 3, get 4 → 3004 = ₹75 per item
Cheapest to costliest: Shop A < Shop B < Shop C

(ii) Percentage discount = free items ÷ total items received

Shop A: 12 × 100% = 50%
Shop B: 13 × 100% = 3313%
Shop C: 14 × 100% = 25%

(iii) Need 4 items

Shop A: buy 2, get 2 free → pay ₹200. Shop B: buy 3, get 1 free → pay ₹300. Shop C: buy 3, get 1 free → pay ₹300.

(i) A ₹50, B ₹66.67, C ₹75 (A cheapest). (ii) A 50%, B 3313%, C 25%. (iii) Shop A – 4 items for ₹200.
