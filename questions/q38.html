Question

In a laboratory, the number of bacteria increases at 2.5% per hour. Find the number of bacteria at the end of 2 hours if the initial count is 5,06,000.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

2.5% growth means multiply by 1 + 0.025 = 1.025 every hour.
After 1 hour: 5,06,000 × 1.025 = 5,06,000 + 2.5% of 5,06,000 = 5,06,000 + 12,650 = 5,18,650
After 2 hours: 5,18,650 × 1.025 = 5,18,650 + 12,966.25 = 5,31,616.25
Formula: 5,06,000 × (1.025)² = 5,06,000 × 1.050625 = 5,31,616.25

About 5,31,616 bacteria after 2 hours.
