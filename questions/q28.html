Question

The population of elephants in a national park increased by 5% in the last decade. If the population a decade ago is p, the population now is
(i) p × 0.5 (ii) p × 0.05 (iii) p × 1.5 (iv) p × 1.05 (v) p + 1.50
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Old population = p
Increase = 5% of p = 5100 × p = 0.05p
New population = p + 0.05p = 1.05p

Option (iv): p × 1.05.
