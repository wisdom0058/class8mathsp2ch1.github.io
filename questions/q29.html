Question

Which statement(s) mean the same as “The demand for cameras has fallen by 85% in the last decade”?
(i) Demand now is 85% of a decade ago. (ii) Demand a decade ago was 85% of now. (iii) Demand now is 15% of a decade ago. (iv) Demand a decade ago was 15% of now. (v) Demand a decade ago was 185% of now. (vi) Demand now is 185% of a decade ago.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Take demand a decade ago as 100%.
Fallen by 85% means 85% is gone, so what is left = 100% − 85% = 15%.
So demand now = 15% of the demand a decade ago.

Only statement (iii) means the same.
