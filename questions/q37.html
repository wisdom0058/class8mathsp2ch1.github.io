Question

The population of a city is rising by about 3% every year. If the current population is 1.5 crore, what is the expected population after 3 years?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

A 3% rise means each year the population is multiplied by 1 + 0.03 = 1.03.
After 1 year: 1.5 × 1.03 = 1.545 crore
After 2 years: 1.545 × 1.03 = 1.5914 crore
After 3 years: 1.5914 × 1.03 ≈ 1.639 crore
Formula: 1.5 × (1.03)³ ≈ 1.5 × 1.0927 ≈ 1.639 crore

About 1.64 crore (≈ 1,63,90,000) people after 3 years.
