Question

Workers in a coffee plantation take 18 days to pick coffee berries in 20% of the plantation. How many days will they take to complete the picking for the entire plantation, assuming the rate of work stays the same? Why is this assumption necessary?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

20% of the plantation takes 18 days.
The whole plantation is 100%. 100% ÷ 20% = 5, so the whole is 5 times 20%.
Days for 100% = 5 × 18 = 90 days
Why the assumption? We multiplied by 5 assuming every 20% takes the same 18 days. In real life the rate could change – weather may change, workers may get tired, some may take leave, or some parts may have more berries.

90 days. The assumption is needed because without a constant rate we cannot simply multiply 18 by 5.
