Question

If the length of a rectangle is increased by 10% and the area is unchanged, by what percentage (exactly) does the breadth decrease?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Let the length be L and breadth be B. Area = L × B.
New length = L + 10% of L = 1.1L = 11L10
Let the new breadth be B′. Area is unchanged, so 11L10 × B′ = L × B
B′ = B × 1011 = 10B11
Decrease in breadth = B − 10B11 = B11
Percentage decrease = B11B × 100% = 10011% = 9111% ≈ 9.09%
Easy check with numbers: L = 10, B = 11, area 110. New L = 11, so new B = 110 ÷ 11 = 10; decrease 1 out of 11 = 9.09%.

The breadth decreases by exactly 9111% (≈ 9.09%).
