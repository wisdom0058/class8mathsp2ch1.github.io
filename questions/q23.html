Question

Samson bought a car for ₹4,40,000 after getting a 15% discount. What was the original price of the car?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Let the original (marked) price be ₹y.
Discount = 15% of y, so Samson paid 100% − 15% = 85% of y.
85% of y = 4,40,000 → 85100 × y = 4,40,000
y = 4,40,000 × 10085 = 4,40,00,00085
y ≈ ₹5,17,647
Check: 15% of 5,17,647 ≈ 77,647; 5,17,647 − 77,647 = 4,40,000 ✓

The original price was about ₹5,17,647.
