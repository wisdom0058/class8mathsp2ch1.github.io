Question

Giridhar borrows ₹12,500 at 12% p.a. for 3 years without compounding. Raghava borrows the same amount for the same time at 10% p.a. compounded annually. Who pays more interest and by how much?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Giridhar (12%, no compounding)

Interest per year = 12% of 12,500 = 12100 × 12,500 = ₹1,500
Interest for 3 years = 3 × 1,500 = ₹4,500

Raghava (10%, compounded)

Year 1: 12,500 × 1.1 = 13,750
Year 2: 13,750 × 1.1 = 15,125
Year 3: 15,125 × 1.1 = 16,637.50
Interest = 16,637.50 − 12,500 = ₹4,137.50

Compare

4,500 − 4,137.50 = ₹362.50

Giridhar pays more interest, by ₹362.50 (the higher rate matters more than compounding over just 3 years).
