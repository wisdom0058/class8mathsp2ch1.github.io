Question

The total sales (revenue) of a company was ₹2.5 crore last year. They had a profit margin of 25% (on cost). What was the total expenditure (cost) of the company?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Let the expenditure (cost) be ₹x.
Profit = 25% of x = 25100 × x = 0.25x
Revenue = Cost + Profit = x + 0.25x = 1.25x
1.25x = 2.5 crore
x = 2.51.25 crore = 2 crore
Check: profit = 25% of 2 crore = 0.5 crore; 2 + 0.5 = 2.5 crore ✓

The total expenditure was ₹2 crore.
