Question

A number increased by 20% becomes 90. What is the number?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Let the number be y.
After a 20% increase the new value is 100% + 20% = 120% of y.
120% of y = 90 → 120100 × y = 90 → 1.2y = 90
y = 90 ÷ 1.2 = 75
Check: 20% of 75 = 15; 75 + 15 = 90 ✓

The number is 75.
