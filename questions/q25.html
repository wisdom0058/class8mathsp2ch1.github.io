Question

The price of 1 kg of rice was ₹38 in 2024 and is ₹42 in 2025. What is the rate of inflation? (Inflation is the percentage increase in prices.)
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Old price = ₹38, new price = ₹42
Increase = 42 − 38 = ₹4
Rate of inflation = IncreaseOld price × 100% = 438 × 100%
= 219 × 100% = 20019% ≈ 10.53%

The rate of inflation is about 10.5%.
