Question

An estimated 90% of the world's population lives in the Northern Hemisphere. Find the approximate number of people living in the Northern Hemisphere based on this year's worldwide population (about 8.3 billion).
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

World population ≈ 8.3 billion
90% of 8.3 billion = 90100 × 8.3 billion
10% of 8.3 billion = 0.83 billion, so 90% = 9 × 0.83 = 7.47 billion
In Indian units: 7.47 billion = 747 crore.

About 7.47 billion (≈ 747 crore) people live in the Northern Hemisphere.
