Question

A 65 g chips packet has Potato 70%, Vegetable oil 24%, Salt 3%, Spices 3%. Find the weight of each ingredient.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Total weight = 65 g = 100%
1% of 65 g = 0.65 g – use this to find everything.
Potato = 70% = 70 × 0.65 = 45.5 g
Vegetable oil = 24% = 24 × 0.65 = 15.6 g
Salt = 3% = 3 × 0.65 = 1.95 g
Spices = 3% = 1.95 g
Check: 45.5 + 15.6 + 1.95 + 1.95 = 65 g ✓

Potato 45.5 g, oil 15.6 g, salt 1.95 g, spices 1.95 g.
