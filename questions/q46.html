Question

40% of the students going on an excursion are Grade 8 students and the rest are Grade 9. Among the Grade 8 students, 60% are girls.
(i) What percentage of all the students are Grade 8 girls?
(ii) If 160 students go, how many are Grade 8 girls?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

(i) Percent of Grade 8 girls

Imagine 100 students in all.
Grade 8 students = 40% of 100 = 40; Grade 9 students = 60
Grade 8 girls = 60% of the 40 Grade 8 students = 60100 × 40 = 24
24 out of 100 → 24% of all students are Grade 8 girls.

(ii) Out of 160 students

24% of 160 = 24100 × 160 = 24 × 1.6 = 38.4
Number of students must be whole, so about 38 Grade 8 girls (or 40% of 160 = 64 Grade 8 students, 60% of 64 = 38.4).

(i) 24% (ii) about 38 Grade 8 girls.
