Question

Pairs of quantities are shown below. Write the correct symbol >, <, = in the blanks. Try to do it without calculations.
(i) 50% ___ 5% (ii) 510 ___ 50% (iii) 311 ___ 61% (iv) 30% ___ 13
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

(i) 50% ___ 5%

50 out of 100 is much more than 5 out of 100.
50% > 5%

(ii) 510 ___ 50%

510 is exactly half. 50% is also exactly half (50 out of 100).
510 = 50%

(iii) 311 ___ 61%

3 out of 11 is less than half (half of 11 is 5.5, and 3 is less than that).
61% is more than half.
311 < 61% (in fact 311 ≈ 27%)

(iv) 30% ___ 13

13 = 3313% (we found this in Question 1).
30% is less than 3313%.
30% < 13

(i) > (ii) = (iii) < (iv) <
