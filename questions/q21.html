Question

A clothing shop offers a 25% discount on all shirts. If the original price of a shirt is ₹300, how much will Anwar have to pay?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Marked price = ₹300
Discount = 25% of 300 = 25100 × 300 = one quarter of 300 = ₹75
Price to pay = 300 − 75 = ₹225
Shortcut: paying 100% − 25% = 75% of the price: 0.75 × 300 = ₹225

Anwar has to pay ₹225.
