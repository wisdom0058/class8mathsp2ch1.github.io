Question

The petrol price in 2015 was ₹60 and ₹100 in 2025. What is the percentage increase?
(i) 50% (ii) 40% (iii) 60% (iv) 66.66% (v) 140% (vi) 160.66%
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Old price = ₹60, new price = ₹100
Increase = 100 − 60 = ₹40
Percent increase is always compared with the old value: IncreaseOld price × 100%
= 4060 × 100% = 23 × 100% = 2003%
= 66.66…% ≈ 66.67%

The petrol price increased by about 66.66% – option (iv).
