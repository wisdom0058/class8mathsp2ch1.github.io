1.4 Growth and Compounding

Question

Bank of Yahapur offers interest of 10% p.a. Compare how much one gets if ₹20,000 is deposited for 2 years with compounding and without compounding annually.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Without compounding (simple interest)

Interest each year = 10% of 20,000 = ₹2,000 (same every year)
Interest for 2 years = 2 × 2,000 = ₹4,000
Amount = 20,000 + 4,000 = ₹24,000

With compounding

Year 1 interest = 10% of 20,000 = ₹2,000 → amount = ₹22,000
Year 2 interest = 10% of 22,000 = ₹2,200 (interest on the new amount) → amount = 22,000 + 2,200 = ₹24,200
Formula check: 20,000 × (1.1)² = 20,000 × 1.21 = ₹24,200

Compare

Difference = 24,200 − 24,000 = ₹200

Without compounding ₹24,000; with compounding ₹24,200 – compounding gives ₹200 more.
