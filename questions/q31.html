1.4 Growth and Compounding

← Q30☰ All QuestionsQ32 →

Question

Bank of Wahapur offers interest of 5% p.a. Compare how much one gets if ₹20,000 is deposited for 4 years with compounding and without compounding annually.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Without compounding

Interest each year = 5% of 20,000 = ₹1,000
Interest for 4 years = 4 × 1,000 = ₹4,000
Amount = 20,000 + 4,000 = ₹24,000

With compounding (interest on the growing amount)

Year 1: 20,000 + 5% of 20,000 = 20,000 + 1,000 = 21,000
Year 2: 21,000 + 5% of 21,000 = 21,000 + 1,050 = 22,050
Year 3: 22,050 + 5% of 22,050 = 22,050 + 1,102.50 = 23,152.50
Year 4: 23,152.50 + 5% of 23,152.50 = 23,152.50 + 1,157.63 = ₹24,310.13
Formula: 20,000 × (1.05)⁴ ≈ ₹24,310.13; compound interest = ₹4,310.13

Without compounding ₹24,000; with compounding ≈ ₹24,310 – about ₹310 more with compounding.
