Question

What percentage of the area is occupied by the region marked ‘E’ in the figure? (The big square is 8 × 8 units; the bottom-left quarter is cut by a diagonal into D and E.)
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Total area of the big square = 8 × 8 = 64 square units
The bottom-left square (D + E together) is one quarter of the figure: 4 × 4 = 16 square units
The diagonal cuts this small square into two equal triangles, so E = 16 ÷ 2 = 8 square units
Percentage of E = 864 × 100% = 18 × 100% = 12.5%

Region E is 12.5% of the total area.
