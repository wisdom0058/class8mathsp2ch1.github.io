Question

Fill in the blanks:
(i) 3 is ___ % of 300.
(ii) ___ is 40% of 4.
(iii) 40 is 80% of ___.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

(i) 3 is ___ % of 300

Let the percent be a. Then a100 × 300 = 3
300 ÷ 100 = 3, so a × 3 = 3
a = 3 ÷ 3 = 1. So 3 is 1% of 300.

(ii) ___ is 40% of 4

40% of 4 = 40100 × 4 = 160100
= 1.6. So 1.6 is 40% of 4.

(iii) 40 is 80% of ___

Let the number be y. Then 80100 × y = 40
80% of y = 40 means 10% of y = 40 ÷ 8 = 5
100% of y = 10 × 5 = 50. So 40 is 80% of 50.

(i) 1% (ii) 1.6 (iii) 50
