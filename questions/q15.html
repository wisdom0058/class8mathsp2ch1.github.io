Question

The badminton coach plans the ratio of warm-up : play : cool-down as 10% : 80% : 10%. For a training of 90 minutes, how long should each activity be?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Total time = 90 minutes = 100%
Warm-up = 10% of 90 = 10100 × 90 = 90 ÷ 10 = 9 minutes
Play = 80% of 90 = 80100 × 90 = 8 × 9 = 72 minutes
Cool-down = 10% of 90 = 9 minutes
Check: 9 + 72 + 9 = 90 minutes ✓

Warm-up 9 min, play 72 min, cool-down 9 min.
