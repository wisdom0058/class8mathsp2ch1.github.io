Question

A milkman sold two buffaloes for ₹80,000 each. On one he made a profit of 5% and on the other a loss of 10%. Find his overall profit or loss.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

First buffalo (5% profit)

SP = ₹80,000 = 105% of CP
CP = 80,000 × 100105 = 80,00,000105 ≈ ₹76,190

Second buffalo (10% loss)

SP = ₹80,000 = 90% of CP
CP = 80,000 × 10090 = 80,00,00090 ≈ ₹88,889

Overall

Total CP = 76,190 + 88,889 = ₹1,65,079
Total SP = 80,000 + 80,000 = ₹1,60,000
Total CP is more than total SP, so there is a loss = 1,65,079 − 1,60,000 = ₹5,079
Loss % = 5,0791,65,079 × 100% ≈ 3.08%

Overall loss of about ₹5,079, i.e. about 3%.
