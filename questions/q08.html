Question

Surya made 60 ml of deep orange paint. How much red paint did he use if red paint made up 34 of the deep orange paint?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Total orange paint = 60 ml
Red paint = 34 of 60 ml
First find one-fourth: 60 ÷ 4 = 15 ml
Then three-fourths: 3 × 15 = 45 ml
In percent: 34 = 75%, and 75% of 60 ml = 45 ml.

Surya used 45 ml of red paint.
