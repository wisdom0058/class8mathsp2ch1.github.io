Question

The cost of materials for a chair is ₹475, and the carpenter wants a profit margin of 50%. At what price should he sell a chair?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Cost = ₹475
Profit = 50% of 475 = 50100 × 475 = half of 475 = ₹237.50
Selling price = Cost + Profit = 475 + 237.50 = ₹712.50
Shortcut: SP = 150% of cost = 1.5 × 475 = ₹712.50

He should sell the chair for ₹712.50.
