Question

The population of Bengaluru in 2025 is about 250% of its population in 2000. If the population in 2000 was 50 lakh, what is the population in 2025?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Population in 2000 = 50 lakh = 100%
250% means 2.5 times: 250% of 50 lakh = 250100 × 50 lakh = 2.5 × 50 lakh
= 125 lakh = 1.25 crore

“Is 250% of” means the new value equals 2.5 times the old (125 lakh). If the question had said “increased by 250%”, the answer would be 50 + 125 = 175 lakh.

Population in 2025 ≈ 125 lakh (1.25 crore).
