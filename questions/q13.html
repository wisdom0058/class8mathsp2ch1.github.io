Question

Mariam's bull ate 1 of 2 units on day 1, 2 of 3 units on day 2, 3 of 4 units on day 3, … and on the 99th day ate 99 of 100 units. Represent these quantities as percentages. What do you observe?
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Day 1: 12 × 100% = 50%
Day 2: 23 × 100% = 2003% ≈ 66.7%
Day 3: 34 × 100% = 75%
Day 4: 45 × 100% = 80%
Day 9: 910 × 100% = 90%
Day 19: 1920 × 100% = 95%
Day 99: 99100 × 100% = 99%

On day n the fraction is nn + 1. The bull always leaves exactly 1 unit, but the total keeps growing, so the leftover becomes a smaller and smaller share.

The percentage eaten increases every day and gets closer and closer to 100%, but never actually reaches 100%.
