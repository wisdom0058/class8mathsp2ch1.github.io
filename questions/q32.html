1.4 Growth and Compounding

Question

Do you observe anything interesting in the solutions of the two questions above? Share and discuss.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

In both questions, without compounding the interest is the same every year, so the amount grows by the same amount each year (like adding).
With compounding, each year's interest is calculated on a bigger amount, so the interest itself grows every year (like multiplying).
So, for the same rate and time, the amount with compounding is always more than without compounding.
The difference grows with time: after 2 years at 10% it was ₹200; after 4 years at 5% it was about ₹310, even though the rate was lower.

For the same principal, rate and time, compounding always gives more interest, and the gap keeps increasing as the number of years increases.
