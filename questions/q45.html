Question

What is 5% of 40? 40% of 5? 25% of 12? 12% of 25? 15% of 60? 60% of 15? What do you notice? Make a general statement and justify it using algebra, comparing x% of y and y% of x.
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

5% of 40 = 5100 × 40 = 200100 = 2
40% of 5 = 40100 × 5 = 200100 = 2
25% of 12 = 25100 × 12 = 3
12% of 25 = 12100 × 25 = 3
15% of 60 = 15100 × 60 = 9
60% of 15 = 60100 × 15 = 9
Notice: each pair gives the same answer.

Why (algebra)

x% of y = x100 × y = x × y100
y% of x = y100 × x = y × x100
Since x × y = y × x (multiplication can be done in any order), the two are always equal.
Useful trick: 8% of 50 is hard, but 50% of 8 = 4 is easy!

x% of y = y% of x for all numbers x and y.
