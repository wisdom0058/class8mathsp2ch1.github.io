Question

The monthly percentage change in a lab's mice population: Month 1 +5%, Month 2 −2%, Month 3 −3%. Initial population is p. Which statements are true?
(i) after 3 months = p × 0.05 × 0.02 × 0.03 (ii) = p × 1.05 × 0.98 × 0.97 (iii) = p + 0.05 − 0.02 − 0.03 (iv) = p (v) more than p (vi) less than p
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

A +5% change means multiply by 1 + 0.05 = 1.05.
A −2% change means multiply by 1 − 0.02 = 0.98.
A −3% change means multiply by 1 − 0.03 = 0.97.
Population after 3 months = p × 1.05 × 0.98 × 0.97
1.05 × 0.98 = 1.029; 1.029 × 0.97 = 0.99813
So the population = 0.99813p, which is slightly less than p.
Why not simply 5 − 2 − 3 = 0%? Because each percent is taken of a different (changed) amount, so the changes do not exactly cancel.

Statements (ii) and (vi) are true.
