Question

Jasmine invests amount p for 4 years at 6% p.a. Which expression(s) describe the total amount after 4 years when compounding is not done?
(i) p × 6 × 4 (ii) p × 0.6 × 4 (iii) p × 0.6100 × 4 (iv) p × 0.06100 × 4 (v) p × 1.6 × 4 (vi) p × 1.06 × 4 (vii) p + (p × 0.06 × 4)
🔊 Audio explanation (step by step)

Solution

Without compounding, interest each year = 6% of p = 6100 × p = 0.06p
Interest for 4 years = 0.06p × 4 = 0.24p
Total amount = p + interest = p + (p × 0.06 × 4) = 1.24p
Check the others: (i)–(v) do not use 0.06 correctly. (vi) p × 1.06 × 4 = 4.24p, which is wrong – multiplying by 1.06 four times over would be compounding, and multiplying by 4 makes it 4 times too large.

The solutions PDF marks (vi) as also correct; that is a mistake – 1.06 × 4 = 4.24, not 1.24.

Only (vii) p + (p × 0.06 × 4) is correct.
